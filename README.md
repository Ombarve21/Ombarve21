UK Crime Analysis

PostgreSQL SQL

Question: What happens to reported crime across three UK police forces?

Loaded, profiled and cleaned 3.4M police records (June 2024 to June 2026) through a staged PostgreSQL ETL pipeline.
Used window functions to keep only the latest outcome for 207,674 updated records, avoiding double counting.
Found only 6.5% of crimes led to a charge and anti-social behaviour fell 11.8% year on year, pointing to seasonal resource planning.
UK Rail Recovery Analysis

R tidyverse ggplot2

Question: Did UK rail journeys recover differently within regions and across borders after the pandemic?

Wrangled multi-sheet ORR datasets in R and tested differences with one-way ANOVA, Tukey HSD and linear regression.
Found a 5-16% shortfall in cross-border journeys despite four years of above-median fare rises, pointing to structural rather than fare-driven causes.
